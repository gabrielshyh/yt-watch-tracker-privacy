YT Watch Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

YT Watch Tracker uses your YouTube viewing activity to show your watch time, viewing patterns, and watch history.

What the extension records

After you enable tracking, the extension records the IDs and available titles of YouTube videos and Shorts you watch, when and for how long they play, and when they reach the end. Watch time is counted while a YouTube tab is visible. The extension saves these records and your tracking choice in local extension storage in this browser. Nothing is recorded before you enable tracking.

Storage permissions

The extension asks for two permissions. storage lets it save your watch history in this browser. unlimitedStorage removes Chrome’s default size limit on that local storage, so your history can keep growing over months and years without saves failing. Both apply only to storage on this device. Neither lets the extension send data anywhere.

How the data is used and shared

The extension uses your records to calculate and display your stats. Watch categories are guessed from video titles on your device and are not sent anywhere. It does not upload your watch-history records to the developer, sell them, or use them for advertising. When the dashboard shows a video thumbnail, your browser requests the image from YouTube’s image host, i.ytimg.com, using that video’s ID. YouTube receives the video ID as part of that image request.

Retention and deletion

Watch history stays in this browser. To permanently delete it, open the extension’s full stats page and choose Reset all watch-time stats under Data & privacy. Tracking then starts from zero. Removing the extension deletes all of its local storage.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

YT Watch Tracker’s use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements.
