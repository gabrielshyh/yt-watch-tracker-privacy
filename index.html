YT Watch Tracker

Privacy policy

YT Watch Tracker uses your YouTube viewing activity to show your watch time, viewing patterns, and watch history.

What the extension records

After you enable tracking, the extension records the IDs and available titles of YouTube videos and Shorts you watch, when and for how long they play, and when they reach the end. Watch time is counted while a YouTube tab is visible. The extension saves these records and your tracking choice in local extension storage in this browser.

How the data is used and shared

The extension uses your records to calculate and display your stats. It does not upload your watch-history records to the developer, sell them, or use them for advertising. When the dashboard shows a video thumbnail, your browser requests the image from YouTube’s image host, i.ytimg.com, using that video’s ID. YouTube receives the video ID as part of that image request.

Retention and deletion

Watch history stays in this browser until you remove the extension, which also removes its local extension storage. You can view your saved watch history from the extension’s full stats page.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

YT Watch Tracker’s use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements.
